The design agent

Reasoning you can audit, not a black box.

DuoSim does not trust a model's first answer. Each design is planned, generated against retrieved worked examples, checked by deterministic engineering rules and by the CAD kernel, and, in deep mode, reviewed by a vision model looking at the actual render.

  • Plan first: equipment list, dimensions, assumptions, and open questions for you
  • Retrieval of matching equipment patterns (cyclones, kilns, fans, tanks, filters…)
  • Geometric critic: floating parts, open pipe ends, duplicates, scale against the plan
  • Best-of-N candidates and repairs that are never allowed to make the design worse
  • Exact kernel verification plus visual judgement of the render

FAQ

Questions engineers ask

Is the output real CAD or just a picture?

Real CAD. Every design is built in FreeCAD (OpenCASCADE) and can be downloaded as STEP, as a native .FCStd document or as a mesh. Each part is measured after the build and compared with the spec.

Which AI models are used? Can I use my own?

The platform runs Gemini, Claude and OpenAI models. On Pro and above you can add your own keys for OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint such as OpenRouter, Groq, vLLM or Ollama. Keys are encrypted at rest, and design calls that use them cost fewer credits.

What CAD formats can I import?

STEP, FreeCAD .FCStd, STL, OBJ and DXF out of the box, plus IGES and BREP when a FreeCAD worker is attached. Parametric FreeCAD primitives are imported exactly. Other solids are fitted to boxes, cylinders, cones, tubes and spheres, and the fit error is reported per import.

Is it safe to let an AI generate CAD?

The model never writes code. It writes a strictly validated JSON spec, and only validated numbers reach the CAD kernel. Uploaded files are parsed as data, and FreeCAD documents are never opened as executable documents.

What happens if a generation fails?

If the model provider or the CAD worker fails, the credits are refunded automatically and the refund shows in your ledger.

Can we run DuoSim on-premise?

Yes. The Enterprise plan covers private deployment (Docker or systemd), SSO and custom simulator plug-ins.
