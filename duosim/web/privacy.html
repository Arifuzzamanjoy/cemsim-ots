What we collect

  • Account data: email address, name, password hash (scrypt), sign-in times and IP addresses of sessions.
  • Workspace data: prompts, reference images, uploaded CAD files, generated specs, CAD exports and renders.
  • Billing data: plan, credit ledger, and the customer reference of our payment processor (card data never reaches our servers).
  • Security data: an audit log of security-relevant actions.

Why

To provide the Service (contract), to bill it (contract), to keep it secure and prevent abuse (legitimate interest), and to meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal data and do not use your content to train models.

Sub-processors

The model provider you select (Google, Anthropic, OpenAI or the endpoint you configure) receives the prompt and images of a request. Our payment processor (Stripe) handles payments. Email delivery uses the configured SMTP provider. The operator must list hosting and other sub-processors here.

Retention

Account and workspace data are kept while the account exists. Deleting your account erases your user record and the workspaces you own alone, including their designs. Billing records are kept as long as tax law requires.

Your rights

You can export your data (Account › Profile › Export) and erase your account at any time. Depending on your jurisdiction you also have rights to access, rectify, restrict and object, and to complain to a supervisory authority. Contact: privacy@duosim.io (replace with the operator's address).

Security

Passwords are hashed with scrypt, API keys and session tokens are stored only as SHA-256 hashes, provider keys are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, and sessions use HttpOnly cookies with CSRF protection.

Cookies

We use one strictly necessary session cookie (duosim_session) and no tracking cookies.
